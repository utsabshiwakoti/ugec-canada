UGEC Canada is a Canadian education consultancy based in Halifax, Nova Scotia. We help students and individuals explore education and career opportunities across Canada.

UGEC works with educational institutions and training providers in different Canadian provinces. Program availability, tuition, admission requirements and intakes can change and should be confirmed with the institution.

What we do

  • Educational consultation
  • Program and college selection
  • Admission and application support
  • Document checklist and organization
  • Application follow-up
  • Student and college communication support
  • Career-focused program guidance
  • Skilled-trades program exploration
  • Support for students already in Canada
  • Referral to authorized immigration professionals
