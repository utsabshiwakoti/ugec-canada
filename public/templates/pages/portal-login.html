How it works

  1. Share your personal referral link, or ask people to choose your name in "Who referred you?"
  2. When they contact UGEC Canada, they appear on your private page
  3. Follow their progress, from first contact to enrolment, and your commission

Your page shows each person's first name and last initial, what they asked about and how far they have got. Their contact details and messages stay private.
