Study permit pathways

We can help with

PGWP to study permit

On a Post-Graduation Work Permit and planning to study again? We help you choose a program and prepare your study permit application.

LMIA to study permit

Working in Canada on an LMIA-based work permit? We help you find a suitable program and plan your move to a study permit.

SOWP to study permit

On a Spousal Open Work Permit? We help you compare programs and prepare your study permit application.

SOWP-eligible programs

Programs that can allow your spouse or partner to apply for an open work permit while you study.
